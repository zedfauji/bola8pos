<objective>
Review, per export, the phase's two highest-risk files.

`src/shared/lib/domain.ts` (2164 lines) and `src/shared/lib/edge-function-contracts.ts` (1358 lines) carry roughly 192 of the phase's line-level findings between them — about a fifth of the total, concentrated in two files. Both are registries: CLAUDE.md names `domain.ts` the single source of truth for domain types and instructs that entity types be inferred from its Zod schemas rather than hand-written, and `edge-function-contracts.ts` is the declared API contract for every Supabase edge function.

A registry exports a wide public surface on purpose. knip's unused-export detection is reachability from entry points, so on a file whose job is to expose more than any single consumer needs, its findings are weakest exactly where they are densest. That is why this file pair gets its own plan with per-export verification instead of riding along in a layer sweep.

Purpose: delete what is genuinely dead in the two registries without cutting either half of a schema/type pair or a contract for a still-deployed endpoint.
Output: per-export dispositions, a smaller registry surface where justified, and a green toolchain.
</objective>
