<objective>
Author the four read-only reporting RPCs that need no audit action: migrate the two
existing client-side reports (voids, hourly→peak-hours) to bounded server-side RPCs and
build the two new aggregation RPCs (modifier-popularity, payment-methods). This promotes
`get_caja_report`'s SECURITY-DEFINER bounded-RPC pattern as the canonical shape for every
date-ranged report, converting the last client-side unbounded report queries (SC-4).

Purpose: SC-1 (RPCs shipped) + SC-4 (no unbounded queries). Each RPC is a near-verbatim
copy of `get_caja_report`'s skeleton with a `p_from`/`p_to` bounded WHERE on an indexed
`created_at`. The two risky spots are baked into the must_haves: modifier-popularity MUST
aggregate `unnest(modifier_ids)` in an isolated subquery (Pitfall 4 — flat-join
double-counts), and payment-methods MUST carry the Phase-23 `status IS DISTINCT FROM
'reopened_void'` exclusion (omitting it re-introduces the revenue-inflation bug).
Output: 3 migration files. NOT pushed here — the single BLOCKING db push is Plan 05.

Note on the "charts-data" 6th RPC (resolves RESEARCH.md Open Question 1): NO separate
`get_charts_data` RPC is built. The Recharts widgets (peak-hours bar, modifier bar,
payment donut) consume these three report RPCs' rows directly. The 6 report RPCs of SC-1
are: peak-hours, voids, deletions-pre, deletions-post (Plan 04), modifier-popularity,
payment-methods — "charts-data" is satisfied by the chart-bearing report RPCs themselves,
not a 7th endpoint (YAGNI — a composite endpoint returning no new data would be
duplication).
</objective>
