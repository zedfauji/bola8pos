Concrete consequences of proceeding, which the developer should confirm they accept:
- `OfflineQueueProcessor` replays queued `start-pool-timer` actions on reconnect. Everything already shipped by 07-09 (both functions live, `pg_net` enabled) is independent of this decision and stays regardless.
