Migrate the two `wPanels`-namespace widgets that build money strings by hand — the caja dashboard and the payment form — moving the currency symbol out of the translation catalogs and into the shared formatter.

Output: 2 migrated widgets, 2 catalogs with 7 keys de-dollared each, 1 new translated key.
</objective>
